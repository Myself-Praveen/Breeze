Why choose Breeze?

🤖

Agent Mode

Sit back as Breeze takes control of your mouse and keyboard to completely automate repetitive desktop workflows.

🎙️

Voice Commands

Trigger actions instantly using your voice. Breeze listens, processes intent natively, and executes commands silently.

🎵

Media Integration

Native deep integration with Spotify and YouTube Music. Just say "Play lo-fi beats" and let Breeze handle the rest.

👁️

Screen Awareness

Breeze runs ultra-fast local OCR (Optical Character Recognition) to read the text on your screen in real time.

🎨

Premium UI

A completely overhauled glassmorphic interface that floats cleanly above your taskbar without getting in the way.

🔒

Privacy First

Powered by local models (like Llama 3) and Tesseract OCR so your screen data never has to leave your computer.
