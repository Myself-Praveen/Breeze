Core Features

Agent Mode

Breeze takes control of your mouse and keyboard to autonomously complete repetitive tasks on your desktop.

Voice Commands

Trigger actions instantly using your voice. Breeze processes intent natively and executes commands silently.

Media Integration

Deep integration with Spotify and YouTube Music. Say "Play lo-fi beats" and let Breeze handle the rest.

Screen Awareness

Ultra-fast local OCR (Optical Character Recognition) allows Breeze to read text on your screen in real time.

Premium UI

A minimalist interface that floats cleanly above your taskbar without interrupting your workflow.

Privacy First

Powered by local models (Llama 3) and Tesseract OCR so your screen data never leaves your machine.
